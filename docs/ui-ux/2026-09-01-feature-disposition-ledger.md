## Scope decision

The first implementation slice is deliberately presentation-heavy: responsive
six-tab rail, one primary Add/Capture action, Today hierarchy/defaults, Focus
start-first order, Todos redundant FAB removal, and Settings duplicate-header
cleanup. Habits, Workout, Calories, Plan, and Review receive disposition and
follow-up evidence; their data and domain contracts remain unchanged until the
slice is validated on Android.
