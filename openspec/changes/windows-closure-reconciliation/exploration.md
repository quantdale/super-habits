## Decisions carried into proposal

1. Keep `final-certification-closure` as the canonical ledger; do not duplicate its 22 tasks into another status store.
2. Make local review/reporting independent of live SQL availability; review repository safety and evidence gaps now, leaving live assertions explicitly blocked.
3. Scope any flow adjustment to Android/profile behavior. Preserve the iOS command path and all meaningful parse/review/confirmation/lifecycle assertions.
4. Require final-source native proof, raw/reviewed classification traceability, complete flow coverage, and exact APK identity. The later commands after the failed tap also need execution.
5. Split the terminal claims: Windows engineering can be exhausted while overall certification remains `NOT CERTIFIED` due to recovery absence.
6. Correct active J8 normative drift during apply, preserving every number and threshold. Historical failure is not a current unresolved optimization task.
7. Avoid a self-referential SHA trap: any report-bookkeeping commit creates a new tip and needs its own CI; publish the final immutable SHA/run attestation after CI without another repository commit.
8. Planning occurs on `docs/windows-closure-proposal`; later canonical execution follows the existing main-only/fast-forward rule. No campaign execution, push, archive, production SQL, or iOS work occurred here.
