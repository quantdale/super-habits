## Why

`external-blocker-closure` is checked 27/27 and still intentionally unarchived at `NOT CERTIFIED`. Green docs-only CI on `575c403`, a passing disposable battery, and historical Android evidence do not close production Scope-7 integrity, the 10/13 iOS result, the preserved 878 ms J8 miss, or current-source Android proof. A successor is required so later execution closes only those residuals without restarting proven work or recording an owner gate as a pass.

## What Changes

- Add a successor certification contract that resumes from current `main` (`575c4035e7b48e9d84df47f7a5f28dfcb14d7e27`), stays on `main` only, and keeps `external-blocker-closure` unarchived until its closure semantics are actually met. Checkbox completion is not certification.
- Require production Scope-7 schema and historical manifest posture to be established from direct evidence, or left explicitly owner-blocked with a recovery packet. A disposable numeric pass is not production proof. Incident cleanup stays a separate approval boundary.
- Require the three remaining iOS flows to be classified from captured evidence before any product or flow edit, then recertified at 13/13 on a later exact SHA. A historical Android APK does not certify drifted source.
- Require the preserved J8 miss (878 ms versus the 800 ms ceiling) to be rechecked only under credible host resources. The ceiling stays 800 ms. A low-memory deferral is not a pass.
- Do not make this certification depend on default-on AI, store submission, a gap-21 policy choice, or enabling anonymous production auth.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `final-certification-closure`: Successor evidence, main-only operation, preservation of the predecessor and foreign stash, continuation past owner gates, and the archival predicate.

### Modified Capabilities

- `backup-completeness-v2`: Production Scope-7 schema and historical manifest certification require direct evidence and must not be inferred from repository SQL or a disposable pass.
- `native-release-qualification`: Current-source iOS must reach 13/13 after classification of the three open flows; current-source Android is certified or honestly environment-blocked. Historical binaries do not certify drifted source.
- `release-candidate-closure`: The preserved J8 miss is not closed by raising the 800 ms ceiling or by treating the older headroom scenario as permission to move the threshold.
